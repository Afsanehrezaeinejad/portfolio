01 / ABOUT

Quality is a design decision.

I work best where product, engineering, and delivery meet.

I’m a Senior QA Engineer at MoneyLion, building AI-powered test frameworks and API mocking layers for backend and platform teams.

Before that, I spent more than three years at Deriv testing wallets, authentication, and the systems around them. I like joining the design conversation before the first line of code is written—because preventing a defect is better than automating around one.

My work combines practical automation with systems thinking: stable pipelines, realistic mocks, useful performance signals, and test strategies that give teams faster feedback.
